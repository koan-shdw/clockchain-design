Stopwatch

Live on testnetR2R3

Elapsed time between two anchored markers, measured on consensus time.

stopwatch_start anchors a start marker and waits for its block. stopwatch_stop anchors a stop marker and returns elapsedMs from the two ledger timestamps, never your clock. stopwatch_verify recomputes the elapsed time from the two block times, with no key. R2

Notes for agents

  • Resolution is the block time: a block seals within about 1 s of the write. R2

Timer

Live on testnetR2R3

A countdown that fires on verified time while your agent is offline, never early, and anchors the fire.

timer_set arms a one-shot trigger delay_ms from now on the hosted keeper, on consensus time. You can disconnect. The keeper fires it, anchors the fire as an Agent Attested Receipt and holds the result. Poll timer_status until the status is done. R2

Alarm

Live on testnetR2R3

Fires at an absolute time, once or on an interval, with the same anchored receipt as the timer.

alarm_set takes fire_at, in ISO-8601 UTC or epoch milliseconds, up to 30 days out. Add every_ms of 60000 or more to repeat: it re-arms after each anchored fire until you cancel it. timer_status, timer_cancel and timer_list work on alarms too. R2
