Shadow · What it does for you

Something to check, instead of a screenshot.

If people have to trust work your agents did (a client, a reviewer, your own team next quarter), Shadow gives them a record they can check themselves.

01

Every step, in order

The task, each tool call and result, each message. Steps are numbered and chained, so a missing or reordered step shows up.

02

One link to share

Close the session and you get a viewer link. The person you send it to opens it in their own browser. No account, no install.

03

Changes show up

Change one character in a recorded step and the verifier's content check fails. The animation above shows a real case.

04

Works with the agent you have

ChatGPT, Claude, Claude Code, Codex or any MCP client. Agents that run code can sign in with their own key, with no person and no account.

05

Fingerprints on a ledger, not your content

The full record stays on the Shadow server. Only hashes go to the ledger, so the ledger holds no step content.

06

You stay in control

Say "stop logging" to stop. Revoke a link, delete a record or delete the whole account at any time, through the same tools.

What a pass does and doesn't tell you.

A pass means the record is intact: nothing added, removed or changed since it was signed. It does not mean the agent was right.

A pass tells you

  • Nothing was added, removed or changed since the record was signed.
  • Steps are in the order they were recorded. A missing or reordered step shows up.
  • Each step happened inside a time window between ledger receipts.

It doesn't tell you

  • That the agent's output is true, or that anything is compliant.
  • Anything the agent didn't record. Hidden reasoning and other apps aren't captured.
  • Exact moments. Times are windows, not timestamps.

Where it is today

  • Anyone with a viewer link can read the whole record, so record synthetic or redacted content.
  • Sign-up was tested in real accounts with ChatGPT, claude.ai, Claude Code and Codex on 20–21 Nov. Other clients and some ChatGPT plans aren't confirmed.
